
# 📱 Habit Tracker

> An intuitive and lightweight web app to track your daily habits and sleep quality right from your phone.

🚀 **[Try the Live] https://renzosantelli11-ship-it.github.io/Habit-Tracker/

## ✨ Key Features

* 🔄 **Unlimited Habit Tracking:** Track as many daily habits as you want with no limits.
* 🌙 **Sleep Tracker:** Monitor your sleep stages (Total, Deep, Light, and REM sleep).
* 📅 **Calendar & Schedule:** Stay organized with an integrated activity planner.
* 🔒 **Privacy First:** Your data is stored safely and locally on your device.
* 📱 **Mobile-Optimized:** Designed specifically to run smoothly on smartphones.
* 🆓 **100% Free:** No ads, no paywalls, and no hidden subscriptions.
